About RAMATA

A Congolese company committed to technological sovereignty.

Founded in 2022 in Lubumbashi, RAMATA Sarl has quickly established itself as a major player in the technological landscape of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Our company was created with a clear vision: to position the DRC as a technological hub in Central Africa.

Over the years, we have developed strong expertise in emerging technologies and built a team of talented researchers and engineers dedicated to innovation.

  • Innovation, Excellence, Integrity
  • Technological sovereignty of the continent
  • Local team of researchers and engineers

Vision & Mission

The strategic objectives that guide each of our projects.

Our Vision

To be the African leader in technological innovation, recognized for the excellence of our solutions and our positive impact on the economic development and technological sovereignty of the continent.

Our Mission

To design and deploy advanced technological solutions adapted to African challenges, while developing local expertise and creating sustainable innovation ecosystems in the DRC and the region.

Our Objectives

To develop local expertise in advanced technologies, promote the technological sovereignty of the DRC, contribute to the country's digital transformation and create economic value by generating skilled jobs.
