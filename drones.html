Overview

A fleet designed for the realities of the continent.

Our drones are developed taking into account the logistical, climatic and security constraints specific to the DRC and Central Africa. Each aircraft is designed to be robust, easy to deploy and maintain in the field.

  • Resistance to tropical climate conditions
